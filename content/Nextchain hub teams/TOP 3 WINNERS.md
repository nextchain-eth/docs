## 🥇 1st Place — 🏃‍♂️ The Great African Run

**Verifiable Athlete Records & Event Infrastructure**

🌐 **Website:** [https://thegreatafricanrun.com/](https://thegreatafricanrun.com/)

🎥 **Showcase Video:**  
👉 _[Insert The Great African Run showcase video link here]_

### Team Overview

The Great African Run is a Cardano-aligned platform focused on building transparent, verifiable, and athlete-centric infrastructure for long-distance running events and athlete records across Africa.

Rooted in Ethiopia’s global legacy in athletics, the project connects athletes, event organizers, and communities through trusted digital records that preserve performance history and recognition.

### Why It Matters

Across Africa, athletic achievements are often stored in fragmented, offline, or trust-based systems. Athlete histories and race results are difficult to verify, preserve, or share beyond individual events.

This limits long-term recognition, fairness, and global visibility for African athletes.

### Cardano Alignment

Cardano acts as the trust layer enabling:

- Verifiable athlete identities
    
- Immutable event and performance records
    
- Transparent recognition and rewards
    
- Sustainable, low-cost infrastructure suitable for Africa
    

### Regional Role

- **Local (Ethiopia):** Empowers athletes and event organizers with trusted records
    
- **Regional (Africa):** Connects events and athlete data across borders
    
- **Global:** Showcases African athletic excellence through verifiable systems
    

### Current Status

- Team roles clearly defined
    
- Project validated through CATS workshops
    
- Documentation in progress
    
- Preparing structured prototype and submission

## 🥈 2nd Place — 🎬 Kirar

**Web3 Creator Platform for Movies, Music, Books & NFTs**

🎥 **Showcase Video:**  
👉 _[Insert Kirar showcase video link here]_

### Team Overview

Kirar is a Cardano-based Web3 creator platform enabling artists, filmmakers, musicians, and writers to publish, monetize, and distribute content directly to their audiences.

The platform bypasses traditional gatekeepers by using NFTs and smart contracts to ensure transparent ownership, royalties, and community participation.

### Why It Matters

Creators across Africa and emerging markets face:

- Limited monetization access
    
- Centralized platforms that extract value
    
- Opaque royalty distribution
    

Kirar introduces a decentralized content economy where creators retain control and earn fairly.

### Cardano Alignment

Kirar leverages Cardano for:

- NFT-based content ownership
    
- Smart-contract royalty distribution
    
- Fractional ownership and micro-investment
    
- Sustainable, low-cost transactions
    

### Regional Role

- **Local:** Empowers African creators with direct monetization tools
    
- **Regional:** Bridges African, Asian, and Arab digital content markets
    
- **Global:** Positions African content within decentralized global platforms
    

### Current Status

- Working website and testnet validation
    
- Strong creator-driven vision
    
- Early adoption signals from local users
    
- Preparing for beta iteration

## 🥉 3rd Place — 🪪 MuluCard

**Digital Identity & Credential Verification**

🌐 **Website:** [https://www.mulucard.com/](https://www.mulucard.com/)

🎥 **Showcase Video:**  
👉 _[Insert MuluCard showcase video link here]_

### Team Overview

MuluCard is a Cardano-aligned digital identity and credential verification platform that enables institutions and individuals to issue, manage, and verify trusted digital credentials.

The project addresses fragmented, paper-based identity systems that limit trust, efficiency, and access to opportunity.

### Why It Matters

In Ethiopia and across Africa:

- Certificates are easily forged
    
- Verification is slow and costly
    
- Individuals lack portable, trusted credentials
    

MuluCard introduces a secure and decentralized trust layer for real-world identity use cases.

### Cardano Alignment

Cardano enables:

- Verifiable, tamper-proof credentials
    
- Decentralized trust without a single authority
    
- Long-term reliability for public records
    

### Regional Role

- **Local:** Improves trust in education and employment
    
- **National & Regional:** Reduces credential fraud
    
- **Continental:** Demonstrates scalable digital identity infrastructure
    

### Current Status

- Roles and execution structure defined
    
- Core use cases validated
    
- Architecture and documentation prepared
    
- Prototype development underway
