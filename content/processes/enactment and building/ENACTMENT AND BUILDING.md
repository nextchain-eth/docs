# 🛠️ Enactment & Building

**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

## Context

Enactment and building mark the transition from learning and discussion into visible action. At this stage, participants are expected to move beyond abstract ideas and begin translating insights from workshops and local context into tangible artefacts.

For NextChain Hub Ethiopia, this phase focused on enabling teams—many of whom were new to Web3—to begin building in practical, accessible ways while maintaining alignment with Cardano and CATS principles.

---

## How Enactment Began

Enactment at the hub started gradually, following onboarding and early ideation. Rather than forcing immediate technical output, teams were encouraged to:

- Clarify their problem context
    
- Identify realistic first implementation steps
    
- Choose appropriate levels of technical complexity
    
- Begin documenting decisions and progress
    

This approach allowed participants to engage with building at a pace that matched their experience while still producing visible progress.

---

## Building Activities

During this phase, teams engaged in a range of building activities, including:

- Defining system architectures and user flows
    
- Identifying Cardano-aligned use cases (identity, provenance, escrow, content ownership, etc.)
    
- Setting up repositories, documentation spaces, and shared tools
    
- Developing early prototypes, testnet experiments, or conceptual models
    
- Preparing pitch narratives and early demos
    

Building was treated as iterative and exploratory, not as final delivery.

---

## Support Structures

To support enactment and building, the hub provided:

- In-person workshops and open working sessions
    
- Virtual calls for clarification and follow-up
    
- Peer-to-peer collaboration and informal mentoring
    
- Continuous guidance on documentation and publishing
    

Given the beginner-heavy participant base, a key focus was helping teams break down complex blockchain concepts into achievable steps.

---

## Observations

Several patterns emerged during enactment:

- Teams progressed more effectively when starting with simple, testable components
    
- Documentation helped clarify thinking and surface blockers early
    
- In-person interaction significantly accelerated understanding and confidence
    
- Technical assistance was a critical dependency for deeper progress
    

These observations informed how the hub adjusted support and expectations in later stages.

---

## Outcomes

By the end of this phase:

- Teams had moved from ideas to early artefacts
    
- Project directions were clearer and more grounded
    
- Participants developed practical familiarity with Cardano concepts
    
- A foundation was established for refinement, reflection, and continuity
    

Enactment and building did not aim for completeness, but for **momentum**, **learning**, and **visible progress**.

---

## Summary

The enactment and building phase enabled NextChain Hub teams to turn learning into action. By prioritizing accessibility, iteration, and documentation, the hub supported participants in taking meaningful first steps toward real-world, Cardano-aligned solutions.
