# 🌱 Grounding & Alignment

**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

## Context

Grounding and alignment focus on ensuring that project ideas and team actions are rooted in real local context and remain aligned with shared programme principles. For NextChain Hub Ethiopia, this phase was critical in moving teams away from abstract or copied ideas toward locally informed, realistic directions.

Rather than starting from predefined problem statements, participants were encouraged to observe, listen, and reflect on their immediate environments, lived experiences, and community challenges.

---

## Grounding in Local Context

Grounding activities took place through:

- In-person workshops and daily interactions with developers
    
- Open discussions about local challenges, constraints, and opportunities
    
- Reflection on participants’ own experiences within education, agriculture, identity, creative industries, and digital access
    

Teams were guided to ask:

- What problems are visible in our daily lives?
    
- Where do trust, access, or coordination break down?
    
- Which challenges are suitable for blockchain-based intervention?
    

This grounding process helped prevent premature solution design and encouraged thoughtful problem framing.

---

## Alignment with Shared Principles

Alignment was reinforced across sessions to ensure that teams:

- Understood the **action-learning** approach of CATS
    
- Focused on learning, iteration, and documentation rather than perfection
    
- Chose solutions appropriate to Cardano’s strengths
    
- Maintained realistic scope given time and skill constraints
    

Hub leads consistently emphasized that alignment was not about uniformity, but about shared direction and values.

---

## Tools and Practices

Grounding and alignment were supported by:

- Repeated workshops and follow-up discussions
    
- Virtual calls to clarify expectations and correct drift
    
- Documentation prompts encouraging teams to explain _why_ before _what_
    
- Peer feedback during pitches and working sessions
    

These practices helped teams refine ideas while staying connected to their original context.

---

## Observations

Key observations during this phase included:

- Teams that revisited context regularly made clearer design decisions
    
- Alignment discussions reduced over-ambitious or mismatched project scope
    
- Grounded projects showed stronger narrative coherence and purpose
    

These insights informed later support strategies and evaluation readiness.

---

## Outcomes

By the end of the grounding and alignment phase:

- Teams articulated clearer problem contexts
    
- Projects demonstrated stronger relevance to local realities
    
- Cardano use cases were better matched to real needs
    
- Teams shared a common understanding of programme expectations
    

Grounding and alignment laid the foundation for meaningful enactment, reflection, and continuity.

---

## Summary

Grounding and alignment ensured that the work emerging from NextChain Hub Ethiopia was not only technically motivated, but context-aware and purpose-driven. This phase strengthened coherence across teams and enabled more responsible and effective building in later stages.
