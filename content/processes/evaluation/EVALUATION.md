# 📊 Evaluation

**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

## Context

Evaluation marked the transition from active building into structured review and recognition. Rather than treating evaluation as a single judging moment, NextChain Hub Ethiopia approached it as a continuation of the action-learning process—focused on learning progression, visible effort, and alignment with programme principles.

Evaluation was designed to be transparent, grounded in documented evidence, and appropriate to a beginner-heavy participant base.

---

## Evaluation Approach

Projects were evaluated based on a combination of qualitative and process-oriented criteria, including:

- Clarity of problem context and grounding in local realities
    
- Alignment between problem, solution, and Cardano use cases
    
- Evidence of learning, iteration, and adaptation
    
- Consistency of participation and engagement
    
- Visibility of progress through documentation, pitches, and updates
    

Technical completeness was considered, but not treated as the sole indicator of success.

---

## Evaluation Inputs

The evaluation process drew on multiple sources of evidence:

- Team pitches and feedback sessions
    
- Project documentation and hub stories
    
- Submitted project update forms
    
- Observations from workshops and in-person sessions
    
- Participation consistency across the programme timeline
    

This multi-input approach ensured that evaluation reflected the full journey rather than a single snapshot.

---

## Local Hub Evaluation

At the hub level, teams were reviewed collectively by hub leads, focusing on learning progression and alignment rather than competition alone.

As a result, **three teams were identified as top performers** within NextChain Hub Ethiopia.

📅 **Local Hub Winners Announcement:** January 4  
🏆 **Top 3 Teams:**

1. MuluCard
    
2. Kirar
    
3. Africa Great Run
    

These teams demonstrated strong grounding, thoughtful use of Cardano concepts, and consistent engagement throughout the journey.

---

## Link to Broader Programme Evaluation

Local hub evaluation feeds into the broader CATS programme evaluation process.

📅 **Key Milestones:**

- January 4 – NextChain Hub Top 3 announcement
    
- January 6 – Local hub recognition window
    
- January 9 – Global top teams announcement
    

This sequencing ensures continuity between local learning journeys and global programme outcomes.

---

## Role of Evaluation in the Process

Evaluation served multiple purposes:

- Recognizing effort and learning progression
    
- Creating clear checkpoints for reflection
    
- Providing closure to one phase while enabling continuity
    
- Informing next steps for teams and the hub
    

By embedding evaluation within the broader process, NextChain Hub ensured it reinforced learning rather than ending it.

---

## Summary

Evaluation at NextChain Hub Ethiopia was not an endpoint, but a reflective milestone. By focusing on learning, alignment, and visible progress, the evaluation process upheld the core principles of the CATS action-learning journey while creating space for continued development beyond formal announcements.
