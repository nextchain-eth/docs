# 🧭 Orientation

**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

## Context

Orientation marked the formal entry point into the CATS 2026 action-learning journey for NextChain Hub Ethiopia. Its purpose was to establish a shared understanding of how the programme works, what is expected of participants, and how learning, building, and documentation are connected.

Orientation was not treated as a one-time event, but as a continuous process reinforced through workshops, virtual calls, and follow-up discussions.

---

## Establishing Shared Understanding

During orientation sessions, participants were introduced to several foundational principles:

- The programme is an **action-learning journey**, not a traditional hackathon
    
- Progress is demonstrated through **visible artefacts and documentation**, not intention alone
    
- Learning emerges through **doing, reflecting, and iterating**
    
- Participation requires consistency, engagement, and accountability
    

These principles were repeated across sessions to ensure clarity, especially for participants new to Web3 and blockchain development.

---

## Orientation Activities

Orientation took place through:

- Virtual introduction calls
    
- In-person workshops and briefings
    
- Telegram-based follow-ups and clarifications
    

Activities included:

- Overview of the CATS structure and timelines
    
- Introduction to Cardano and relevant Web3 concepts
    
- Explanation of documentation, publishing, and evaluation expectations
    
- Guidance on team formation and collaboration
    

---

## Supporting Beginner Participants

Given that many participants were beginners in blockchain development, orientation placed emphasis on:

- Reducing intimidation around technical concepts
    
- Encouraging questions and open discussion
    
- Providing beginner-friendly resources and learning paths
    
- Reinforcing that learning progress matters as much as technical depth
    

This approach helped participants remain engaged rather than discouraged.

---

## Outcomes

Orientation resulted in:

- Clear understanding of programme expectations
    
- Improved readiness for workshops and building phases
    
- More intentional participation and team formation
    
- Reduced confusion around roles, timelines, and evaluation
    

Participants entered later stages with greater confidence and alignment.

---

## Summary

Orientation created the shared foundation necessary for effective action-learning. By consistently reinforcing expectations and principles, NextChain Hub Ethiopia enabled participants to engage with the programme in an informed, confident, and structured way.
