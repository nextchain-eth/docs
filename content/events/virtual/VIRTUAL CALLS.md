**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

As part of the CATS 2026 programme, **NextChain Hub Ethiopia** organized a series of in-person workshops to onboard developers, introduce the action-learning approach, and support early team formation. These sessions brought together university students and independent developers into shared learning spaces.

---

## In-Person Workshops

**Dates:** November 5 & November 8, 2025  
**Format:** In-person workshops  
**Audience:** University students and local developers  
**Attendance:** Approximately 15–20 participants per session

### Workshop Context

- **One workshop** was conducted **inside a university**
    
- **Other sessions** were held as **open in-person workshops**, with participants coming from universities and the wider developer community
    

This hybrid approach allowed the hub to reach both academic and non-academic developers while maintaining continuity across sessions.

---

### Purpose

The in-person workshops were designed to:

- Introduce the CATS 2026 Hackathon and action-learning methodology
    
- Onboard participants to Cardano and Web3 fundamentals
    
- Ground project ideas in local context and lived experience
    
- Encourage organic team formation through repeated interaction
    

---

### Activities

- Overview of the CATS 2026 programme and expectations
    
- Introduction to Cardano concepts and real-world use cases
    
- Explanation of documentation, publishing, and evaluation requirements
    
- Open Q&A, discussion, and informal team formation
    

---

### Outcomes

- Strong engagement from developers with university backgrounds
    
- Most active teams formed during or immediately after these workshops
    
- Improved baseline understanding of Cardano among beginner participants
    
- Sustained daily engagement over nearly two weeks through in-person sessions
    

These workshops created a reliable bridge between learning, collaboration, and team formation within the hub.

## Summary

The in-person workshops formed the foundation of NextChain Hub’s mobilisation strategy. By combining university-hosted and open community workshops, the hub enabled consistent participation, supported beginner onboarding, and created the conditions for effective team formation aligned with the CATS action-learning model.
