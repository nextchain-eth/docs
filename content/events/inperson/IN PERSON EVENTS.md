# 📍 In-Person University Workshops

**NextChain Hub Ethiopia – CATS 2026 Action-Learning Journey**

As part of the CATS 2026 programme, **NextChain Hub Ethiopia** conducted in-person university workshops to onboard developers, introduce the action-learning approach, and support early team formation. These workshops created direct, daily engagement with students and early-stage developers and became the primary entry point for participation in the hub.

---

## University Workshops

**Dates:** November 5 & November 8, 2025  
**Format:** In-person workshops  
**Audience:** University students and early-stage developers  
**Attendance:** Approximately 15–20 participants per session

### Purpose

The university workshops were designed to:

- Introduce the CATS 2026 Hackathon and its action-learning methodology
    
- Onboard participants to Cardano and Web3 fundamentals
    
- Create space for problem discovery rooted in local context
    
- Encourage early ideation and organic team formation
    

---

### Activities

- Overview of the CATS 2026 programme and expectations
    
- Introduction to Cardano, blockchain basics, and real-world use cases
    
- Explanation of action-learning, documentation, and publishing
    
- Open discussion, Q&A, and informal team exploration
    

---

### Outcomes

- Strong engagement from university developers
    
- Majority of active teams emerged directly from these workshops
    
- Improved understanding of Cardano concepts among beginners
    
- Sustained daily interaction with developers over nearly two weeks
    
- Established a reliable pipeline from learning to team formation
    

These workshops proved essential in grounding the programme within the local developer ecosystem and enabling consistent participation beyond virtual calls.

## Summary

The in-person university workshops served as the foundation of NextChain Hub’s community mobilisation. By meeting developers where they already learn and collaborate, the hub created sustained engagement, supported beginner onboarding, and enabled meaningful team formation aligned with the CATS action-learning approach.
